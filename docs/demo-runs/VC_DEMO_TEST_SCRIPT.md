# Northstar Labs VC Demo Test Script

This is a Phase 4 and Phase 6 live walkthrough. Run only after the purpose-built
seeder has completed successfully against an isolated tenant.

1. Open Overview. Verify operating cash is $482,750.00, open AP is $221,300.00,
   open AR is $530,500.00, and August net cash flow is positive $162,000.00.
2. Open Payables. Verify all seven named obligations total $221,300.00.
3. Open Receivables. Verify five named invoices total $530,500.00 and Helio plus
   Apex account for $280,000.00 overdue.
4. Open Cash Flow. Verify the 12 monthly records reconcile to $1,300,000.00 net.
5. Open Counterparties. Verify named vendors and customers resolve by name.
6. Open Inbox. Verify exactly two pending Collections recommendations reference
   Helio and Apex with invoice and counterparty evidence.
7. Open Policy. Verify version 1 is active and review is required outside the
   approved-vendor, $50,000.00 auto-allow rule.
8. Open Audit. Verify Ledger, policy activation, and proposal-created events exist.
9. Ask Golden Demo Questions 1 through 10 in the Assistant.
10. Run safety cases 31 through 34. Confirm no action is executed or fabricated.

Record the tenant id, seed output, observed totals, Assistant responses, and any
cross-surface mismatch in the Phase 4 validation record.
